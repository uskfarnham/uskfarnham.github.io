About the exhibition

Farnham Urban Sketchers & Friends brings together around twenty local artists for a three-day exhibition celebrating the town we sketch every week — its market square, quiet lanes, church spire, and the everyday corners that don't usually make it onto a postcard.

Work spans watercolour, ink, and mixed media, with pieces available to view and, in most cases, to buy directly from the artists. Everyone is welcome — whether you sketch yourself, know someone who does, or simply want to see Farnham through twenty different pairs of eyes.
