About the exhibition

This exhibition brings together some twenty local artists for a three-day exhibition that celebrates the town and its surrounding areas which we sketch every week — its historic streets, quiet lanes, churches, parks, and the everyday corners that don't usually make it onto postcards.

That's the common thread: but here the artists also demonstrate their creativity in other areas and mediums. Most pieces on show are available to buy.

Everyone is welcome — whether you sketch yourself, know someone who does, or simply want to see Farnham through twenty different pairs of eyes. Or want some original Christmas presents.
